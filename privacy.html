Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2023


At Ngami Theatre Activists (N.T.A), accessible from www.ngamitheatre.org, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by N.T.A and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you fill out the contact form on our website. This includes:

  • Name
  • Email Address
  • Message content

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to communicate with you regarding your inquiries, events, and membership opportunities. We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your Personally Identifiable Information to outside parties.

3. Cookies

Our website does not use cookies to track personal data for marketing purposes. We may use cookies for basic website functionality (like remembering your preferences) to enhance your user experience.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about our Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

Email: info@ngamitheatre.org
Location: Maun, Ngamiland, Botswana
